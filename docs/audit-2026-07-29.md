### What the redesign is

- **A powder ground with colour plates** (item 57, in `Home.tsx`): welcome and
  Dr. Nupur on powder; how a visit goes on cobalt; inside the clinic on powder;
  the treatments on canary; parent voices and questions on powder; the brushing
  game on coral; booking and the footer on cobalt. Sections of one colour run
  together with no rule between them. Each change of colour is `<SmileEdge>`:
  the outgoing ground dips into the next in the curve of the tagline unit's smile
  (p14). No artwork crosses a section's top or bottom edge (item 56).
- **No boxes.** Content sits on the ground. The only framed things are the
  concept photographs, in the guide's p34 image-tile treatment, and the map.
  Coral carries no text anywhere: the game's title is set on a canary ellipse
  (p33–34) and its clock inside a cobalt disc, the disc the roundel is printed on.
- **The book's pages, one per chapter.** p16 (a powder field with a giant canary
  loop) for the welcome, set with p34's copy and dashes. p4's cobalt chapter page
  for the visit, carrying p3's glyph row in canary and white. p35's type on a
  path ringing the heart and the smiling face. p32's move of scaling an
  illustration up into a field (the canary toothpaste behind the FAQ heading, the
  toothbrush at 33% on coral). A cobalt page with a canary loop for booking.
  p36's roundel to close. Every asset on `ART` has a use on the home page.
- **Motion is two systems, never more:** *draw* (the hero loop, doodles, glyphs,
  CTA outlines and the journey thread) and *reveal* (text rising line by line out
  of a clip mask, via SplitText, which ships free with GSAP 3.13+). The journey's
  canary thread is scrubbed to the scroll, and each glyph draws as the thread
  reaches it. The hero's entrance waits for the preloader (`onIntroDone`), and
  `EASE.celebrate` still belongs to the timer's finish alone.
- **Mobile first, one component per section.** Every section is a single
  responsive component; the desktop layout is the same content arranged as the
  book's spreads (text left, art right; a sticky heading beside the journey).
